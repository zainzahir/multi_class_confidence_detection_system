Email Sync & Classification

Automatically fetch and classify student emails to determine their confidence levels. The system connects to configured email accounts, reads student messages, and runs each through the confidence detection model.

🔄 How it works

  • Connects to the configured email server (IMAP)
  • Fetches unread emails from registered students
  • Runs each email body through the confidence classification model
  • Stores predictions linked to the respective student accounts
  • Results appear in the Prediction History tab with "Email" source badge
